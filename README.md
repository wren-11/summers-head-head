# Summer's Head Head · Summer的人头子

by Summer头子

**English** · [中文](#中文)

A tiny tracing table for drawing beginners. Put a reference picture underneath, set how faint it is, and trace right on top to study head structure and proportion, for example with the Loomis method. No drawing software or layers to learn.

## What it does

- Choose a picture (or drag / paste one in), set its opacity, or hide it for a moment
- One pencil with pressure and paper grain, in black, blue and red, plus an eraser; each remembers its own size, and a ring shows the size before you touch down
- Shape correction: pause at the end of a stroke with the pen still down and a loop becomes a circle or ellipse, a line goes straight; keep moving to resize and rotate, pause again to stretch or squash
- A 3D Loomis head you can turn, with the eyes, nose tip, chin and ears marked, and drop onto the page to trace over (it can be bigger than the picture; face lines are drawn heavier than the back)
- An overview in the corner: the whole picture with a box for what the screen shows; tap or drag it to jump there
- Undo / redo, pinch to zoom and twist two fingers to rotate the page on tablets (R straightens it), ⌘+ / ⌘− on a computer with a pen tablet
- Save as PNG, with or without the reference; on tablets, save straight to Photos
- Nothing is uploaded: your drawing stays on your own device
- Chinese and English, switchable at any time

**Open it:** https://wren-11.github.io/summers-head-head/

## Install it on a tablet

Open the link once and add it to your home screen. It then opens full screen like an app and works offline.

- **Android** (e.g. Wacom MovinkPad): open the link in Chrome → ⋮ menu → “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”
- **iPad**: open the link in Safari → Share button → “Add to Home Screen”

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Keys | Action |
|---|---|
| ⌘ + / ⌘ − / ⌘ 0 | Zoom in / out / fit |

## 中文

给绘画初学者练头部结构和比例用的小工具，原理就像绘画用的透写台：选一张参考图垫在下面，调好透明度，直接在上面描，比如做 Loomis 头部分析。打开就能用，不用安装绘画软件，也不用学图层。

### 功能

- 打开后选一张图（也可以拖进窗口或粘贴），调整原图透明度，或者暂时隐藏原图
- 一支铅笔（有笔压和纸纹颗粒感），黑 / 蓝 / 红三种颜色，以及橡皮；铅笔和橡皮各自记住粗细，落笔前能看到大小圆圈
- 形状校正：画完先别抬笔，停半秒，圈会变成圆或椭圆、线会变直；继续不抬笔移动可以缩放、旋转，再停一下可以拉伸 / 压扁
- Loomis 头部参考：可以 3D 旋转观察，标出了眼睛、鼻尖、下巴和耳朵的位置，也能放到画面上对着描（可以超出图片边缘；脸部线条粗、后面的线条细）
- 左上角缩略图：显示整张图和当前屏幕看到的范围，点或拖动就跳过去
- 撤销 / 重做，双指缩放、双指转动画布（平板，按 R 或点角度按钮摆正），⌘+ / ⌘− 缩放（电脑 + 数位板）
- 保存为 PNG，可以选择要不要包含参考图；平板上可以直接存到相册
- 不会上传任何内容，画的东西只留在你自己的设备上
- 中英文界面，随时切换

**打开网址：** https://wren-11.github.io/summers-head-head/
